# Listing Data Collection Template

Use this checklist when collecting real listings from WhatsApp groups, Facebook groups, dealers
and competitor sites. Collect into a spreadsheet with these exact columns, then enter via the
site's Sell / Post Rental form (or bulk import later). Columns marked ★ are required by the
site's listing form; the rest dramatically improve listing quality, SEO ranking and buyer trust.

> Legal note: never copy listing *text or photos* from competitor sites (copyright + duplicate
> content kills SEO). Use competitor listings only to find the dealer's phone number, then call
> them, get permission, and collect fresh details/photos directly. Listings collected with the
> dealer's consent are your originals.

## Practical workflow that works

1. Join 10–15 active property WhatsApp/Facebook groups for your target city.
2. When a dealer posts a property, message them: *"Assalam o Alaikum — main MedaGhar.com se
   hoon, hum aap ki property FREE list kar dete hain photos ke saath, calls seedha aap ke number
   par. Details bhej dein?"* — almost nobody refuses free exposure.
3. Fill one spreadsheet row per property; chase the photos (the #1 bottleneck).
4. Post via /sell or /post-rent the same day; send the dealer their live link — they will share
   it in groups themselves, which markets the site for you.
5. After their listing gets views/calls, pitch the PKR 1,500 Featured upgrade (see /pricing).

Target: 5 listings/day = 100+ real listings in 3 weeks, plus a contact book of dealers who are
your first paying customers.
